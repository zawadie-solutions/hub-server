ZAWADIE — COMPANY WEBSITE FACTS (zawadie.com)
Source: zawadie.com (homepage, /about-us/, /services/, /our-impact/, /contact-us/)

1. WHAT ZAWADIE IS
Zawadie is a social enterprise / outsourcing (BPO) company that connects skilled refugees with remote work opportunities for global businesses. Core value proposition as stated on the site: "Zawadie connects refugees to remote jobs simply and sustainably" by focusing on work that doesn't require formal work permits, operating within legal frameworks.

2. COMPANY HISTORY
Founded: March 2023, in Los Angeles, California.
November 2023: Opened an office at Ubuntu in Dowa, Malawi, hiring 13 refugees for remote roles in lead generation and web development.
January 2024: Founder Tyler Clark visited Dzaleka Refugee Camp in Malawi to meet the initial team, marking the transition from concept to operational reality.

7. ENGAGEMENT MODEL / PROCESS
Zawadie's site describes a three-step client engagement process:
1. Discovery Call — initial consultation to understand the client's objectives and obstacles (scheduled via Calendly).
2. Team Assembly — building a dedicated team and testing deliverables against a sample before full execution.
3. Delivery — execution prioritizing quality and turnaround, with clear milestones and transparent communication tied to the client's success metrics.
The site emphasizes customized workflows over standardized/off-the-shelf packages. No specific published pricing/rate figures are listed publicly on the site — pricing is handled via the Discovery Call.

8. BUSINESS CASE / CLIENT BENEFITS (as stated on site)
- Employee turnover around 10%, versus a 15-20% industry average.
- Labor cost reductions of up to 45% for clients.
- Teams can be deployed in as little as one week.
- Flexible, scalable staffing options.

9. IMPACT (as stated on /our-impact/)
- 200+ refugees have achieved self-sufficiency since 2024.
- $1 million in annual labor cost savings delivered to clients (cumulative claim on the impact page).
- Average Zawadie employee earnings are described as 3-10x the global extreme poverty line (defined on the page as $1.90/day); by contrast, camp residents are described as typically surviving on about $7.60/month, with 99% lacking adequate resources.
- Framing used on the site: "every time a refugee is hired, it transforms far more than just one life — it lifts an entire family," citing downstream access to education, nutrition, healthcare, and entrepreneurship opportunities for the hired refugee's household.

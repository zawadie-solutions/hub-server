About Ombeni AI
Ombeni AI is Zawadie Solutions' internal company assistant. It answers questions and gives recommendations grounded in the Zawadie knowledge base and every recorded meeting, and can create action items directly in Monday.com. Slack and the Monday.com Notetaker connect here once their API credentials are added to the server's environment.

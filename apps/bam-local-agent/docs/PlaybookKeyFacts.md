## Q2 2026 Playbook Overview
The Q2 2026 Marketing Playbook covers March 30 to June 28, 2026 (ISO-8601 weeks 14 to 26; weeks run Monday to Sunday, so months overlap a few days). Corporate provides: static and video social templates, event and flyer templates, the national calendar, national ad spend, event guidelines and product ordering reminders. The franchisee (store) provides: localization of content, posting local social content and replying to comments, local ad spend, in-store flyer printing/display/distribution, email campaigns to local markets, and choosing incentives, specials or coupons. The companion "2026 Event Playbook" holds the detailed event guides, social/email templates, make & take instructions and activity ideas (Q2 event ideas on its pages 15-22). Templates live in Canva or Google Drive. Do Right Now (start today): determine your spring break dates and prep an event; choose in-store events and contact community partners; source parts for make & take builds and promos; focus Easter marketing in March because Easter is April 5; source glass vases for the free-vase promos; source 3M Command Strips or Hooks for the Built for Any Home promo.

## Spring Break Build (campaign, spring break dates vary by location)
Campaign goal: host an in-store event during your local spring break. This campaign has no separate revenue or passport target. Primary audience: mothers ages 35-44. Secondary audience: kids ages 5-11 (elementary students are the main reason parents come in; if the kids have a great time they ask to come back). Core message: turn break time into build time. Make fun family memories during spring break; give your kids something creative and screen-free to do while school is out; pick out something for yourself because you've earned a break too. Parents are invited to bring kids for an hour or more of fun, and a LEGO-fan parent may also buy something for themselves. Call to action: sign up for a spot (link to your event sign-up page). Event ideas, make & take instructions and templates are in the Event Playbook. Related campaign running at the same time: Built for Any Occasion (March 30 - June 28) launches in stores on Monday April 6, which is the first day of many spring breaks (for example April 6-10 in Cobb County, GA and Alpine School District, UT); its goal is $42,000 per store in new-in-box LEGO sets ($14,000 per month, about 375 sets per month), so spring break visitors are a chance to sell NIB sets as well as fill event spots. Built for Any Home starts Monday April 13 with a Botanicals/LEGO Art focus and a free vase (Botanicals $35+) or free 3M Command Strips/Hooks (LEGO Art) promo.

## Built for Any Occasion (campaign, March 30 - June 28, 2026) - revenue goal
Goal: $42,000 total revenue per store from new-in-box (NIB) LEGO sets over the quarter, which is $14,000 per month or an average of 375 NIB sets sold per month per store. Company-wide total: $9.66 million. Audience: gift givers, parents and grandparents. Message: a new LEGO set marks any occasion or milestone (Easter, graduations, birthdays, parties, Mother's Day, Father's Day, family trips, or just because); BAM has a wider selection of new sets than ever and big and small sets for any occasion. Call to action: get directions (link to your Google Business Profile). Local calendar: launch the campaign Monday April 6 with in-store signs and flyers as bag stuffers or register handouts. To finish the quarter strong and hit the NIB goal, run a NIB sale and/or an event on Saturday June 27. Sub-campaigns: Built for Any Home, Grad, Mom, Dad, Birthday, Kid, Trip.

## Built for Any Home (sub-campaign, starts Monday April 13, 2026)
Targets adults looking for unique decor: Botanicals, LEGO Art and fandom display sets. Reason: spring cleaning and summer moves make people refresh their home or need housewarming gifts. Suggested promotions: free vase with a Botanicals purchase of $35 or more (source glass vases from a thrift store or in bulk from Amazon or an event planner); free pack of 3M Command Strips or Hooks with the purchase of a LEGO Art set. Calendar: start the sub-campaign Monday April 13; post about your promos Tuesday April 14; host a home-decor build event Saturday April 18 (build a small decoration), with a Story reminder the day before.

## Built for Any Grad (sub-campaign, May 2026, graduation dates vary by location)
Targets people gifting to graduating students: university grads, high school grads and students who simply worked hard this school year. A new LEGO set is a unique way to say congratulations and fills some well-earned summer free time. Determine graduation dates for your area and adjust timing to your market; the calendar launches the sub-campaign on Tuesday May 12 by sharing NIB gift ideas to reward grads (adjust the date for your market). Monthly sub-campaign goals roll up into the $42,000 per-store NIB goal.

## Built for Any Mom (sub-campaign, May 5-10, 2026; Mother's Day is Sunday May 10)
Dad and the kids are looking for a gift for mom; Botanicals are a natural fit, but there is a set for moms who don't like flowers. Suggested promotions: free vase with a Botanicals purchase of $35 or more; free Mother's Day minifig stand with a Minifig Maker purchase. Event idea: a Mother's Day make & take where dad and the kids build a small gift for mom (calendar: Saturday May 9). Calendar: print signs and flyers Friday April 24; begin the Mother's Day promotion with your Botanicals promo Friday May 1 (Mother's Day is the following week); host the make & take and share a last-minute gift reminder Saturday May 9.

## Built for Any Dad (sub-campaign, June 2026; Father's Day is Sunday June 21)

## This Is Where the Fun Begins - May the 4th weekend (May 1-4, 2026)
Revenue goal: $12,100 total per store for May 1-4 (company-wide total $2.783 million). May the 4th is one of the highest-earning weekends of the year. Primary audience: Star Wars fans ages 18-44; secondary: Star Wars fans ages 5-17 (adults may bring kids). Message: the galaxy's greatest destination for LEGO Star Wars fans; find LEGO Star Wars sets; celebrate as a family; connect with other local fans. Call to action: sign up for a spot; get directions. Suggested promotions: free BAM "May the 4th" minifig stand with a $35+ purchase; free Star Wars poly bag set with a $50 purchase if you have stock. Calendar: Monday April 20 post signs and flyers; Thursday April 30 reminder that the celebration begins tomorrow; Friday May 1 Star Wars Weekend begins; host an event Saturday May 2 or Monday May 4; Wednesday May 6 one more day of promos to move remaining materials.

## Star Wars wording rules (legal - applies to all copy)
NEVER use: "Star Wars Day"; "May the Fourth be with you"; "May the Force be with you"; "A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away..."; "The Force"; "This is the Way"; "Dark Side" or "Light Side"; "Jedi", "Sith", "Padawan" or "Droid"; names of any Star Wars character, vehicle, species, faction, location or item (unless part of a product you sell, e.g. "This Millennium Falcon set includes a Han Solo minifigure"); film music/audio, clips, still frames or memes from them; the official Star Wars logo. SAFE to use: "May the 4th" or "May the Fourth"; "LEGO Star Wars"; "Star Wars fans" or "Fans of Star Wars"; "Join the Brick Side"; images of products you sell and official LEGO Group product images (LEGO E-tailer Guidebook rules still apply). Costumed characters are for customer entertainment only: they may not sell or promote products, hand out merchandise or giveaways, or point to products or places of business, and employees should not wear Star Wars costumes while working.

## Time to Build. Time to Win. - MOC Wall Clock contest (May 15 - June 14, 2026)
Product: Santoki's new licensed LEGO wall clock; BAM is one of the exclusive launch partners. Store goal: sell 10 MOC Wall Clocks per store (company-wide total 2,300 clocks). This is NOT the passport campaign. Contest: at some point during the contest period set out demo clocks so customers can decorate them with bulk bricks and enter; Santoki provides signage and instructions; kids can enter with a parent or guardian's permission; the most creative builders win prizes from BAM and Santoki, including a $550 BAM gift card as the grand prize. Primary audience: AFOLs ages 18-44 (LEGO fans with display space tapped out who can still hang a clock); secondary: DIYers ages 18-34 and kids ages 8-17. Message: get a hands-on look at the new LEGO clock; have fun building plus a chance to win; experiment with your design before building one at home. Call to action: check out the prizes (link to the contest page). Calendar: announce the clock in stock, including the contest, on Friday May 15.

## Put Bricks & Minifigs on the Map - BAM Passport (June 1-28, 2026)
Store goal: sell or gift 25 BAM Passports per store (company-wide total 5,750 passports). This target belongs to the passport campaign in June, not to Spring Break or May the 4th. Passports are not free but are sold at a low price and may also be used as a gift with purchase. Stamp design is largely up to the store but needs marketing-department approval; passport rewards are set and fulfilled by corporate. Message: with summer here, traveling families can collect unique stamps at every BAM store; become a BAM superfan; earn unique BAM swag with enough stamps; earn and redeem loyalty points on one account at any location. Primary audience: AFOLs ages 18-44 (they likely already have a LEGO Store passport); secondary: kids ages 5-11 (who will ask to visit other BAM locations). Call to action: check out the swag (link to the passport page); get directions. Calendar: Thursday May 28 print passport and World Play Day flyers and post and email a teaser; Saturday May 30 announce the passport arrives next weekend; Friday June 5 passport launch day; Saturday June 6 summer-kickoff event promoting the passport; Monday June 22 feature the passport again for summer travel.
